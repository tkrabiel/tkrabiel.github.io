📖 How to read the NOAA table: Look for rows M2, S2, N2, K1, O1, P1.
• Amplitude – enter in FEET (we convert to meters).
• Phase – use the value under "Phase (GMT)" in degrees.
⚠️ If P1 is missing, estimate it as K1 amplitude × 0.33 with similar phase.
🕒 All times and phases are referenced to GMT.
