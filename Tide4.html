📐 The geometry of diurnal tides

Tide‑generating forces act symmetrically, but declination (angle above/below equator) breaks the twice‑daily symmetry.

🌕 Lunar declination varies from -28.6° to +28.6° over 18.6 years (due to Moon's 5.1° orbital inclination + ecliptic obliquity).
☀️ Solar declination ranges ±23.5° annually.

When the Moon or Sun is off the equator, the two tidal bulges are not aligned with the equator. A point on Earth rotates through unequal highs — that's diurnal inequality.

Constituents born from declination: K₁ (luni-solar declinational), O₁ (lunar declinational), P₁ (solar declinational).

🌊 How declination creates diurnal constituents

The equilibrium tidal potential expands into harmonics. Declination introduces terms with periods of ~24h (diurnal). The main ones:

  • K₁ – Luni-solar declinational (23.93 h)
  • O₁ – Principal lunar declinational (25.82 h)
  • P₁ – Principal solar declinational (24.07 h)
