NASA SUMMARY

Solar Maximum vs Solar Minimum

Solar Maximum (NOW)

The Sun’s magnetic field is highly active. Sunspots, solar flares, and coronal mass ejections become frequent. More extreme space weather events. Auroras visible farther from the poles.

Cycle 25 • Peak declared Oct 2024 Double-peak possible

Solar Minimum (last in 2019)

The Sun is quiet. Few or no sunspots. Magnetic activity is low. Fewer solar storms. The Sun appears almost spotless in visible light.
